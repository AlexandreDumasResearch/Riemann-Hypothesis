## Research summary

This 107-page manuscript investigates RH-related arithmetic criteria and continuation for the Riemann xi function using signed prime sums, pole-neutral Chebyshev–Laguerre tests, exact moving cutoffs, effective square-summable remainder estimates, two-prime correlation forms, theta-source positivity, and residual/heat/discriminant dynamics.

The central explicitly stated unconditional decomposition takes the form
```text
U_m + h_m = kappa + r_m,   r in ell^2
```
where `U_m` is an ordinary-prime sum and `h_m` a reciprocal-zero diagonal, with the precise definitions and effective bounds given in the manuscript.

The paper distinguishes proved or source-conditional mathematical statements from RH-equivalent **criteria whose decisive arithmetic bounds remain open**. It does **not** establish an unconditional proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
