Signed Prime Correlations and Theta Continuation for the Riemann Xi Function

SUBSTANTIVE MATHEMATICAL ADDITIONS

1. Effective complete-sector extraction. The unchanged remainder now has
   sum_{m>=M}|r_m|^2 <= 3*10^12 M^(-1/2), M>=2. The proof retains
   the prime-square endpoint, gamma, every higher prime power and the
   full moving cutoff. A refined formula allows smaller computable
   constants. The displayed universal constant is intentionally coarse.
   Consequences: weighted l^2 for exponents below 1/2, weak l^(4/3),
   l^p for p>4/3, and complete-sector Cesaro error O(N^(-3/4)).

2. Pointwise extraction. An elementary adjacent-Laguerre differential-
   equation argument gives r_m=O(m^(-3/4) log^(5/2)(m+2)). The gamma
   sector alone has leading term -32/[27(2m+1)]; this is not asserted
   as the leading term of the complete remainder. Finite error bounds
   are provided. No endpoint l^(4/3) or optimal-rate claim is made.

3. Complete cross-degree prime sampling. The centered prime-diagonal
   form equals an exact banded Jacobi form plus a compact operator.
   The sharp symbol range is [64,20000]. The actual-cutoff estimate
   is uniform over arbitrarily wide growing degree blocks, and includes
   an explicit error in terms of the prime discrepancy norm. That norm
   is defined analytically but has not been numerically enclosed.
   This strengthens fixed-lag asymptotics to an operator statement.

4. Independent complete-sector identity. All even prime powers collapse
   exactly into -zeta'/zeta on the zero-free line. Abel continuation
   recovers the endpoint correction and gives an exact Parseval tail
   formula preserving correlations between all auxiliary sectors.
   The unrestricted Bessel constant is proved sharp.

5. Larger complete-theta positivity region. Opposite contour rotations
   give positivity for |x| <= b_n n^(3/2)/(log n)^2, for any b_n -> 0,
   uniformly at bounded heat of either sign. Full contour, endpoint,
   Gaussian and theta-mode estimates are included. Negative-heat
   compatibility remains explicit and prohibits inferring RH from
   this region alone.

PRIORITY AND NECESSARY QUALIFICATIONS

The introduction now explicitly credits Sekatskii's generalized Li and
subexponential criteria, Coffey's Laguerre/von-Mangoldt decompositions,
Suzuki's bounded-response and norm criteria, and Connes--Consani's
finite-Mellin-constraint Weil criterion. Their underlying principles
are not claimed as discoveries here. The contribution claims concern
the precise extraction, quantitative bounds, cross-degree form and
complete-source asymptotics proved in this manuscript. No exhaustive
international-priority guarantee is made.

No fatal proof defect required removal of an inherited theorem. One
potential overinterpretation was explicitly excluded: the sharp diagonal
lower bound is relative to weighted coefficient energy. Broad averaging
can reduce that energy; the theorem does not rule out averaging itself.

REMAINING DECISIVE PROBLEM AND PUBLICATION STATUS

None of the new estimates bounds U_m independently of h_m. The missing
arithmetic estimate remains signed cancellation between distinct primes
sufficient to make the quadratic averages of U_m bounded (or polynomially
bounded). The source alternative still requires the cofinal discriminant
budget or uniform actual Jensen residue signs. RH remains unresolved.

The complete manuscript and reproducibility package are ready for serious
external mathematical evaluation. The new claims have full proofs and
focused reproducible algebra checks, but no numerical score, external
acceptance, comprehensive novelty assurance or RH proof is asserted.
