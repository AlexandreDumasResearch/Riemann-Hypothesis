# RH Research Atlas — Critical Edition (23 November 2026)

**Alexandre Dumas** · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-1695-0086)

**Title:** *In Pursuit of a Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis: From Schur Minors and Heat Collisions to Gaussian Detectors and Signed Primes.*

This critical edition documents several years of Riemann-hypothesis research and preserves both positive results and identified limitations. **It does not claim a complete, independently verified proof of RH.**

## Version and citation

The [original Atlas](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24454608) has Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.24454608**. That DOI identifies the **earlier edition**, not this critical revision. Please cite this revision by its date and repository path unless and until a distinct archived version is published.

**Mathematical status:** Exact finite identities, computational evidence, conjectural pathways, and conditional implications are distinguished throughout. A full proof of the Riemann hypothesis is not established by this manuscript.
