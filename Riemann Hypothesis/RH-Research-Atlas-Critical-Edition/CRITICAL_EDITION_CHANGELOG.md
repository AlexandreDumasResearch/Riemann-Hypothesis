## Material mathematical changes

- Original Paper III: reclassified the fourfold cocircuit as an exact finite **kernel identity**, no longer as proof of the original universal forced-root skeleton. Added the independently recomputed exact counterexample `A_mix(4,4,4) = -13888`, and a distinct numerical control at `A_mix(4,4,10) = 6167456`. The 9 October source reports the corrected all-parameter coefficient-extraction formula, retained with its source hypotheses. Historical Chapter 26 explicitly superseded, preserving the archive.
- Added **A29–A38**: May raw Xi curvature and leading-model saddle margin; October harmonic/discriminant cancellation; October 11 scalar Pringsheim criterion; October 13 Jensen residue and moment conditioning; two nonidentical October velocity spectra; November central-divisor localization; corrected Paper III extraction and counterexample.
- Expanded the primary source table and independent numbered-series bibliography. The original Papers I–IX, referee I–III and II.1–II.9, October velocity papers, and Residual Dynamics volumes are separate families. A September Paper X *readiness* checkpoint is not presented as a completed Paper X; standalone XI/XII sources were not independently recovered from the accessible Library census.
- Updated the publication/research-value index from 37 to **42 entries**. Original Paper III's earlier 85/100 was reduced to 79/100 after its skeleton was disproved; the reported indices are editorial judgments, **not** proof probabilities or external referee grades.

## What has *not* been certified

No new theorem family has undergone outside expert peer review as part of this release; the compiler has not independently reconstructed the 295-page October 11 analytic proofs or 75-page October 13 saddle estimates. The exact finite Paper III regression is **not** a proof of any proposed all-parameter factorization or a mathematical certification of RH. Existing limitations of the external arithmetic-window estimate (W) and of the full Weil-form cofinal bound remain explicit.
