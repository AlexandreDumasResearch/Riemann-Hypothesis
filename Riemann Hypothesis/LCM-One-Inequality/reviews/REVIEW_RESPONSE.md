## Important remaining questions

- Is the precise Mellin-positivity converse correct under every regularity and sign hypothesis used?
- Are the Gaussian growth theorem's score maximization and remainder estimates uniform in the required parameters?
- Can (W) be rigorously obtained from a cited, pinned, formal theorem, with exactly the same quantifiers?
- How much of the equivalent criterion and signed oscillation theorem is already implicit in classical literature?

Until those are resolved, describing the manuscript as a fully certified or independent proof of RH would be incorrect.
