## 2. Exact optimal conductor from the source's matrix-span property (C31)

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Ann}_O(K/T)=\operatorname{Ann}_O(B/R),\quad \operatorname{len}(B/R)=r'\operatorname{len}(K/T).}\tag{C31a}$$
Under source trace-nondegeneracy in characteristic 0, the complete rational algebra is semisimple (its faithful semisimple matrix representation trace form, or radical argument), so K is torsion. ## 3. Saturation obstruction and negative test (C32)
N_sat=H∩(Nτ⊗F). N_sat/Nτ is torsion in Hτ and acts by zero on L* and O. Applying the same tensor exactness gives K/T→K_sat surjective with kernel image of L*⊗_{Hτ}(N_sat/Nτ). Thus product of annihilators of that image and surviving kernel kills K/T. Even if all bad factors are killed over F and the remaining algebra is good-only, the saturation cost need not be small.

Explicit counterexample, ℓ odd, O=Z_ℓ[ζ_ℓ], arbitrary H0≥1. Group generated by tuples of good rank-one character ρ, augmentation 1, and bad 3×3 matrices. All good evaluations 1 except ρ(s)=−1. Bad s=diag(−1,1,1), a=1+E12, b=1+ℓ^H0 E21, c=1+ℓ^H0 E23, d=1+ℓ^H0 E32, τ=diag(1,1,ζ_ℓ). Use γ=s and tie τ. Rational bad algebra is M3(F), since elementary matrices generate it, and its simple factor is distinct from the two good characters. Nτ⊗F is entire bad factor because τ−1 nonzero there and zero on good. H/N_sat is O×O, separated integrally by (1−s)/2. Yet modulo ℓ^H0 the top-left 2×2 blocks of all bad group elements are upper triangular, diagonal (ρ(g),1); the other off-diagonal couplings vanish. The (1,2) entry c0(g) is therefore an obeying cocycle with c0(γ)=c0(τ)=0, c0(a)=1. It has order ℓ^H0, forcing conductor exponent at least H0. Tie is of order ℓ, vℓ(ζ_ℓ−1)=1/(ℓ−1) independent of H0. Rational separation and small eigenvalue valuations alone cannot prove a cheap tied conductor. This is an algebra example, not an actual Galois/Hodge counterexample.
