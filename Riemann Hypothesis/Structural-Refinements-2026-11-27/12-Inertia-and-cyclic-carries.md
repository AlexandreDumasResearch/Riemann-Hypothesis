## 3. Rank-one inertia correction (C47)
Over an absolutely unramified local field K of degree d, enlarge O to contain its embeddings. A crystalline rank-one character with labelled weights n_i has inertia restriction given by the product of embedding powers of units via local reciprocity, up to common sign. This is the standard classification (e.g. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10068827/). For x=exp(ℓ y), y∈O_K, the logarithm of the character is ±ℓ Σ n_i σ_i(y). ## 6. Saturated inertia relations: weaker actual target (C53)

## 7. Why even inertia invariant lifting can be too strong

It has no rational inertia invariants. Embed it in an abstract larger group generated by the local group and an extra free generator with matrix 1+p^H E21; include augmentation character. Rational trivial-factor projector ensures nonzero invariant-lift ideal, but its exponent is arbitrarily costly. Yet for the trivial selected local representation the actual fixed I_w has left inertia action by χ (all matrix entries lie in the first row, augmentation zero). Every equivariant map I_w→O/p^M with trivial target is zero because χ−1 is a unit at some tame element. Hence reached crystalline defect zero for every M. For selected χ itself, I_w is free with scalar action χ, so maps mod p^M lift O-linearly and equivariantly; cocycles are rational combinations of the crystalline z and coboundary, hence crystalline when integral. Thus the high invariant-lift cost is not the actual relation cost. This is a local crystalline/abstract global algebra counterexample, not a family of actual RH automorphic columns or proof that the proposed uniform bound fails for them.
