## 2. Integral idempotent (C15)

$$e=p(a),\quad e^2=e,\quad \epsilon(e)=0,\quad \rho(e)=Id.\tag{C15}$$
This is an exact integral idempotent on the entire algebra, not necessarily central. ## 3. Right augmentation annihilation and Peirce quotient
P=L*⊗_H I, μ(φ⊗i)=φi, K=ker μ. The right H action on P comes from I. For x=Σφ_j⊗i_j∈K and b∈I, x b=Σφ_j i_j⊗b=μ(x)⊗b=0. Thus
$$K I=0.\tag{C16}$$
Put q=1−e, A=eHe, B=eHq, D=qHq, D+=qIq. Since e∈I and L*e=L*, L*q=0, for any x∈P, xe=μ(x)⊗e. Therefore P e≅L*, K=P q. Iq=B⊕D+ as O-modules. The balanced map L*×Iq→L*⊗_A(B/BD+) sending (φ,i)↦φ⊗ei is H-balanced: ehi=ehe ei+ehq qi, with the latter term in BD+; φh=φ ehe. The inverse comes from the inclusion B⊂Iq; BD+ dies by balancing through q. Hence
$$\boxed{K\simeq L^*\otimes_A(B/BD^+).}\tag{C17★}$$
This is an off-diagonal (Peirce) module, not a full trace discriminant. C5 is not needed for the proof. The map of a normalized cocycle row φ⊗(u(g)−1) to K is φ⊗e u(g)q. ## 5. Local generator bound independent of number of columns (C19)
Let S be any finite O-module quotient of P (or of K after the O-linear split projection π_K), and U_w⊂S the span of local evaluation rows z_g(φ), φ∈L*, g∈G_{E_w}. It is finite generated even if S has free rank. Here z_g(φ) denotes the image of φ⊗(u(g)−1), or its projection. Balancing gives z_{gh}(φ)=z_g(φ)+z_h(φρ(g)); this survives O-linear quotient/projection. Each functional U_w→k_O produces a continuous cocycle with values in L/πL. Injectivity follows because evaluation rows generate U_w. Continuity follows from actual source continuity of u_x and the finite O-lattice topology on I and the quotient (a submodule of a finite module has its usual induced π-adic topology). Thus
$$\nu_O(U_w)=\dim_{k_O} U_w/\pi U_w\le\dim_{k_O}Z^1(G_{E_w},L/\pi L).$$
Tate local Euler characteristic gives h^1=h^0+h^2+[E_w:Q_ℓ]r', and coboundaries have dimension r'−h^0. Tate duality gives h²≤r'. Hence
$$\boxed{\nu_O(U_w)\le r'([E_w:Q_\ell]+1)+h^2\le r'([E_w:Q_\ell]+2).}\tag{C19★}$$
A submodule of a finitely generated DVR module has no more minimal generators than the ambient module (Smith decomposition/induction on cyclic summands), so the bound also holds for its reachable crystalline relation submodule, or for its image after ties/tame relations. If c_w annihilates this image, normalized length is ≤ν(U_w)v_ℓ(c_w); use normalized vℓ(ℓ)=1. Sum local degrees 2f, number of places≤2f. For a common exponent bound vℓ(c) across places, total ≤6fr'vℓ(c), independently of the number of column representations or full trace-algebra rank. This uses standard local duality/Euler characteristic, not quantitative crystalline duality; see e.g. https://www.math.ucla.edu/~hida/205a.1.15f/Lec09s.pdf . It is not an actual conductor bound. The noncommutative counterexamples in 03 still show exponent can be arbitrarily large.

## 6. Exact weighted sufficient target (C20)
Let vℓ(c_main,ℓ) and vℓ(c_Tate,ℓ) bound the normalized exponents of the actual local reachable relation images uniformly across required coefficient embeddings, places, lifts and main-pair representations. Put V_main=Σℓ vℓ(c_main,ℓ)logℓ and V_Tate similarly. Using the actual weights (m lifts, good rank r and string t≤T0, k/2 rank-one main pairs per lift counted twice, t_z=2z−1, z=7m/2+1,…,7m/2+k/2):
$$\boxed{\mathfrak A_{\rm crys}\le6fm[(r+k)V_{\rm main}+(t+k(7m+k/2))V_{\rm Tate}].}\tag{C20★}$$
This is sufficient subject to the source interfaces and actual normalized costs. If V_main=O(a log b/(mk)) and V_Tate=O(V_main/T0), with T0=5r∼k, this gives O(fa log b). The target can also use distinct exponents by representation/place rather than maxima. This reduces the global problem to controlling the exponent of the reached torsion, without a large multiplicity from full algebra rank.
