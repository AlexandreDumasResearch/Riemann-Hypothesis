## Important mathematical status

The LCM manuscript proposes an RH-equivalent integer inequality and analytic growth results. Its separate short deduction of RH **depends on an external uniform arithmetic-window estimate (W)**. The exact implication from the Alpöge–Claude formalization to that quantified estimate is not independently verified in this repository. This is **not an independent proof of RH**.

Historical source material and reviewer critiques are distinguished from independently certified results.
